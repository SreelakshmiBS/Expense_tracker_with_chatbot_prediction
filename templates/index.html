The Problem

Why traditional tracking
fails you

Most people discover they have overspent only at month-end — when the damage is already done. Pen-and-paper records, basic spreadsheets, and generic apps share the same fatal flaw: they record the past but offer nothing about the future.

01
Manual entry is slow and error-prone
Recording every transaction by hand is tedious. One missed entry distorts your entire financial picture for the month.
02
No automatic categorisation
Every record must be sorted manually. Patterns stay hidden inside rows of unlabelled numbers.
03
No intelligent insights or guidance
Spreadsheets show you what happened. They never warn you what is about to happen or tell you what to do about it.
04
Overspending goes unnoticed
Without real-time alerts, budget breaches are discovered only after the fact — too late to correct course.
05
Zero prediction capability
Traditional tools cannot forecast whether next month's spending will rise or fall, leaving you flying blind.
